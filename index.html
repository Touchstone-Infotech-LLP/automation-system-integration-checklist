What the framework covers

Use it before implementation, during architecture reviews, or when existing automations become unreliable.

Process & architecture

Business discovery, system inventory, ownership and source-of-truth design.

CRM & lead flow

Lead capture, routing, opportunity stages, appointments and follow-up.

APIs & webhooks

Authentication, event delivery, retries, rate limits and idempotency.

Messaging automation

WhatsApp, email, SMS, consent, preferences and human escalation.

AI-assisted workflows

Human review, prompt controls, sensitive data and fallback paths.

Reliability & security

Logging, monitoring, access control, testing and governance.
